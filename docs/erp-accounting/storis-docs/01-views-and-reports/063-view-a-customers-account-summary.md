Access

Accounting > Receivables > Receivables Views > View a Customer's Account Summary

Page Headings: Details, General

Use this inquiry to view general open item receivables information for the selected customer.

NOTE: The output of this inquiry may be affected by Regional Processing restrictions.

Details

Credit Limit

The customer's credit limit displays here.

Credit History Credit History

The customer's credit history codes display here. It shows how past due a customer's payments are for each given month.

Collector Collector

The assigned collector displays here. This field is initially displayed but may be hidden after a customer is entered, depending on your account settings and available customer information.

Amount Due

Current Current

The amount currently due for the customer.

1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 120+ 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 120+

The amount due for the customer divided into the appropriate aging buckets.

Total Due Total Due

This field displays the total amount due from all the aging buckets.

Account Balances

Revolving Revolving

The total amount of revolving accounts receivables for the customer displays here. This field is hidden if the Revolving AR module is not licensed and active via General System Control Settings.

Installment

Total Due

Complete sum of all current due open item receivables.

Total AR

The long term and short term AR displays here.

Liability

Deposits Deposits

The total amount of the deposit liability.

General

The information displayed here is the View a Customer's General Information page. This topic also contains information about the header.
