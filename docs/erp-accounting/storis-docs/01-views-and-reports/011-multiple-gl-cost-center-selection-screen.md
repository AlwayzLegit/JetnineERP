This window appears when you click on the Action button at the Cost Center field in the GL Account Settings. Use it to link any of the following to the root account selected in the GL Account Settings. If using sub-accounts, the screen links to the selected sub-account.

a single GL Cost Center Settings

multiple GL cost centers

a range of GL cost centers

cost centers associated with one or more districts

cost centers associated with one or more regions

a selected list of GL sub-accounts

Region RegionSpecify one or more regions whose associated cost centers you want to link to the account specified in the GL Account Settings. If you click on the Arrow, a list of regions appears from which you can choose one or more regions. If you click on the Action button, you access the Multiple Region Selection window, from which you can choose one or more regions.

District DistrictSpecify one or more districts whose associated cost centers you want to link to the account specified in the GL Account Settings. If you click on the Arrow, a list of districts appears from which you can choose one or more districts. If you click on the Action button, you access the Multiple District Selection window, from which you can choose one or more districts.

NOTE: Cost centers are associated with regions and districts based on the locations in the selected regions or districts.

Cost Center Cost Center

Specify one or more cost centers. You have the following options:

enter items directly into the Cost Center field

click on the arrow to view a list of GL cost centers from which you can choose

enter the first in a range of accounts at the Cost Center field and enter the last account in the range at the Through field

click on the Actions button the access the List Entry Window, which you can use to select a GL cost center list

Whichever method you use, the items you specify appear in the grid at the bottom of the window.

Through ThroughIf you enter a single cost center at the Cost Center field, this field activates and you can enter a "through" response to indicate the last cost center in a range of cost centers that you want to link to the selected account. If you enter a response here, the response at the Cost Center field becomes the first in the range. If you leave this field blank, the program selects only the response in the Cost Center field.

Grid InformationGrid Information

You can remove items from the grid. To remove all of the items in the grid, click on Delete at the bottom of the window. To remove a single item, double-click on it to select the item and activate the buttons to the right of the entry field. Then, click on Remove to remove the selected item from the grid.

You can use the up and down arrows to navigate the list in the grid. If more records exist than can fit in the grid, you can use the scroll bar on the right side of the grid to view additional records. When you locate the desired record , highlight it and press Enter. You can also double-click on the record to select it.

When the grid contains all of the items you want, click OK. The systems returns to the previous screen. The current field displays "..." to indicate multiple items. To exit the Multiple Selection Window without specifying any products, click on Exit. You return to the previous screen.
