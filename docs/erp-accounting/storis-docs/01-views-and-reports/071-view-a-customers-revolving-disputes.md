Access

Accounting > Revolving Receivables > View a Customer's Revolving Disputes

Accounting > Revolving Receivables > Revolving Views and Reports > Revolving Views > View a Customer's Revolving Disputes

Use this routine to view revolving dispute details for a customer. Once you specify the customer, any orders/transactions in dispute for the customer display in a grid.

CustomerCustomerEnter the code of the customer whose plan/transaction is in dispute. You can click the Search button to access the Search for a Customer routine. Once you have specified the customer code, the customer's name displays.

Home, Work, Cell Home, Work, CellThe customer's home, work, and cell telephone numbers display from customer settings.

CommentsComments

If Account Comments were entered for this customer via Customer Settings, the Comments button is active. When you click the Comments button, you access the read-only Customer Credit Comments window.

Account Balance $ Account Balance $This is the customer's revolving balance.

Previous MMP Due $ Previous MMP Due $This is the last cycled MMP amount.

Total in Dispute $ Total in Dispute $This is the total amount currently in dispute.

Past Due $ Past Due $This is the short term revolving balance that remained unpaid as of the cycle date.

Current Due $ Current Due $The revolving amount currently due from this customer is displayed.

Total Due $ Total Due $This is the total payment amount due for all plans.

Grid InformationGrid Information

The grid displays all dispute transactions for the customer. For each disputed transaction, the grid displays the Plan code, Transaction reference, Posted date, Removed (date to remove dispute), Resolved (date to resolve dispute), Suspend Interest status (None, Total Balance, or Dispute Balance), dispute Amount, and Reason for the dispute. The Removed & Resolved dates and Suspend Interest settings are determined during Update Disputes entry.

To view dispute comments for a transaction, double-click a line from the grid. The View Dispute Comments screen appears.
