Access

Accounting > Receivables > Receivables Views and Reports > Receivables Views > View a Customer's Current Balance Details

Pages: Details, General

Use this inquiry to review open item receivable transactions for a selected customer. The inquiry displays items

with a non-zero balance (open) and

items whose balances became zero after the last Day Ending display.

NOTE: The output of this inquiry may be affected by Regional Processing restrictions.
