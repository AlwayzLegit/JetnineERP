Access

Receivables > Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Forgive Late Fees button

Receivables > Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details > Forgive Late Fees button

Use this routine to forgive one or more late fees in one session. Upon entrance, the grid is populated with all unpaid late fees for the contracts selected from the Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts grid. The grid sorts by contract, then by installment, and assessed date.

NOTE: All late fees in the grid can be selected and deselected by clicking the upper left box in the menu row of the grid.

Comments Comments

Clicking the actions button at this field brings up the Text Entry Screen where you can enter comments pertaining to the contract.

Comments are required when forgiving late fees on a contract.

Grid InformationGrid Information

Late fee $ - The late fee amount associated with a contract displays here.

Assessed - The date the late fee was applied displays here.

Contract - The contract number displays here.

Installment - The installment number and the length of the term displays here. For example, if a late fee was charged on the first payment of a 36 month term, it would display as 1 - 36.

Reference - The reference number associated with the late fee displays here.

Actions

G/L Account Maintenance - Selecting this takes you to the G/L Distribution screen where you can maintain G/L accounts for installment contracts.
