Access

Enter a Sales Order > Payment tab > Entering an ineligible revolving plan at the Financing field

This window displays the reason(s) the selected plan is not eligible for use. A message is displayed for each restriction rule that fails. The restriction rules are maintained by plan on the Restrictions tab in Revolving Payment Plan Settings.

Here are examples of some of the messages that might display:

The minimum deposit of $___ has not been applied to the order.

Minimum credit score restriction exists, customer has no credit score.

The customer's credit score is less than the minimum score required.

Maximum credit score restriction exists, customer has no credit score.

The customer's credit score is greater than the maximum score required.

The customer is greater than ___ days past due.

The plan cannot be used in store ___.
