Global Actions

Adjust Balance - Use this routine to increase or decrease the long-term balance of the revolving plan.

Update MMP - Use this routine to make changes to the principal, finance charge, insurance or late fee amounts on a short-term revolving payment due.

Add New MMP - Use this routine to add payments due to open item receivables and impact the balance due (short-term) on the revolving plan.

Change Details - Use this routine to make adjustments to order details for the selected revolving plan, without impacting the long or short term balance of the plan.

Update Disputes - Use this routine to place either an entire revolving plan, or an individual completed order associated with the plan, in dispute.

Plan Balance Transfer - Once you indicate the customer, this button is active. Use this process to transfer a customer's revolving plan balance to a new plan, or use it to transfer multiple plans to one plan in a single session. To import plans for multiple customers, use the Import Revolving Plan Balance Transfer routine.

You can enter a customer and a plan, or a customer only, to use the transfer feature. If you enter a customer only, and not a plan on this screen, the plan balance transfer process displays information for all of the customer's eligible plans. If a plan was also selected, the process screen displays information for only that plan. To be considered eligible for a plan transfer, the following rules apply.

No portion of the plan balance can be in dispute.

Plan must have a short term or long term balance.

Plan cannot be a master plan.

Plan cannot have a pending balance.

Plan cannot have a payment agreement.

Plan Deferment - Use this routine to select revolving payments due to defer for the selected customer.

This button is available if:

A customer has been selected.

The customer has not been charged off.

The customer has a revolving payment due.

The customer's plan is set to deferment via the Allow Deferment setting in Revolving Payment Plan Settings.
