Access

via the Promise button on the Collector Review - Customer Update Screen.

Use this screen to assign a promise-to-pay (PTP) date and amount to the selected customer. The date and amount you specify here display on the Collector Review - Customer Update Screen and also appear in the customer’s Collections Comments file.

Date Date

Specify the promise-to-pay date. You can enter it directly, or if you click on the Calendar Icon, a calendar appears via which you can select a date. If a PTP date exists, it displays here.

Amount Amount

Enter the promise-to-pay amount, up to a maximum of $99999.99.

Collected Collected

The total promise-to-pay amount collected since the PTP date and amount were first entered display for the customer.

Amount Remaining Amount Remaining

The total promise-to-pay amount remaining displays for the customer.
