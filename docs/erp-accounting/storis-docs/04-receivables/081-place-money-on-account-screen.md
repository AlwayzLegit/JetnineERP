This screen appears via the Actions menu in the Enter a Customer Payment or Maintain Customer Balances routines. Use this screen to place the money being received on the customer's account for future application.

The reference number defaults from the Customer record and you cannot edit it.

Amount Amount

Enter the dollar amount you want to place on account.
