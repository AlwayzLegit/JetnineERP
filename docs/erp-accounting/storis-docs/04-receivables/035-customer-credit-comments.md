Access

Comments button on various revolving entry and view routines, such as

- Adjust Revolving Plans

- Enter a Customer's Revolving Terms & Conditions

- View All Revolving Activity for a Customer

- View a Customer's Revolving Disputes

Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Merge/Refinance Contracts > Actions button > View Customer Credit Comments

Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details > Actions button > View Customer Credit Comments

Use this screen to view the customer's credit comments, entered in the Account Comments field in Advanced Customer Settings.

This screen appears when you click the Comments button found on Revolving Receivables entry and inquiry screens. It can also be found via Actions buttons in Installment Receivables. The comments display in a read-only text box. The comments shown on this screen do not print on customer documents. They are used by sales/credit personnel to view credit information that should be taken into consideration when working with a customer's account.
