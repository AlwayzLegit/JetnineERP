AccessAccess

Installment Receivables > >Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details > Actions button > View Historical Contract Rebates

Use this screen to view interest and insurance rebates on a closed contract.

Grid InformationGrid Information

Type of Charge - This column displays the type of rebate charge.

$ Original - The original amount for the type of charge in that row displays here.

$ Rebate - The rebate amount for the type of charge in that row displays here.

The total line displays the total amount of all original and rebate amounts in the grid.
