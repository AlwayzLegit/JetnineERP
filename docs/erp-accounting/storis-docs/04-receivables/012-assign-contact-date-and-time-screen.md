Access

via the Contact button on the Collector Review - Customer Update Screen.

Use this screen to assign the date and time you want the collector to contact the selected customer. The date and time you specify here display on the Collector Review - Customer Update Screen and also appear in the customer’s Collections Comments file.

Date Date

Specify the contact date. You can enter it directly, or if you click on the Calendar Icon, a calendar appears via which you can select a date. If a contact date exists, it displays here but you can edit it.

Time Time

Enter the contact time in 24-hour format. If you enter 4 digits, you do not have to enter a colon (:) to separate the hours and minutes. For example, to enter a log-in time of 8:34 AM, enter: 0834
