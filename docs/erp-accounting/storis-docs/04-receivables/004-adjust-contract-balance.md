Access

Receivables > Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Adjust Contract Balance button

Receivables > Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details button > Adjust Contract Balance button

Use this routine to increase or decrease the balance of an installment contract and allocate it to the area you specify, such as principal, insurance, or interest. When you make an adjustment, you can view the activity in View Contract Postings.

Contract Contract

The contract number displays here.

Comments Comments

Clicking the actions button at this field brings up the Text Entry Screen where you can enter comments pertaining to the contract.

Comments are required when adjusting a contract balance.

Current Balance Current Balance

The current balance of the installment contract displays here.

Adjustment of Adjustment of

The sum of the adjustment amount(s) entered in the grid below displays in this field. Once a value has been entered in the Adjustment column of the grid, this field calculates.

New Balance New Balance

The new balance amount after adjustments have been made displays here. Once a value has been entered in the Adjustment column of the grid, this field will calculate.

Grid Information
Actions

G/L Account Maintenance - Select this option from the Actions menu to access the GL Distribution Screen, where you can maintain G/L accounts for installment contracts.

Allocations - The standard allocations are listed in this column and consist of: Principal, Interest, and Insurance. Also listed are additional (active) adjustment types, if any, established via Contract Balance Adjustment Settings.

$ Long Term - This column is display-only and is only applicable for standard allocations. The unpaid portion of non-filing fees and the portions of Principal, Interest, and Insurance that have not yet cycled display here. A negative adjustment amount cannot exceed the value of this column.

Adjustment - Use this column to enter a positive or negative adjustment for an allocation.
