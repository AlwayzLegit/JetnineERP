AccessAccess

Installment Worksheet > extra Actions button at APR field

Review Contract Details > extra Actions button at Annual Percentage Rate field

Use this routine to view additional APR information. This window is only accessible if the installment contract has different APR values.

Same as Cash Same as Cash

This field displays the last date that a contract can be paid in which interest and insurance fees are avoided. This date is calculated using the Contracts Paid Within __ Months Qualify Same as Cash setting in Installment Payment Plan Settings.

APR Before Cash Date APR Before Cash Date

This field displays the APR that applies to the principal amount on or before the Same as Cash date above.

APR After Cash Date APR After Cash Date

This field displays the APR that applies to the principal amount after the Same as Cash date above.
