Access

Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Merge/Refinance Contracts button > Actions button > Calculated Average Monthly Insurance Premium

Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details > Merge/Refinance Contracts button > Actions button > Calculated Average Monthly Insurance Premium

Use this calculator to determine the average monthly insurance premium to charge based on the term. The calculation is based on the Rule of 78's. The cost of insurance is available in the Forms Designer for installment contract printing.

Term __ Months Term __ Months

Click the arrow to choose a term length. Selecting a term length recalculates the monthly average.

Monthly Average Monthly Average

This field displays the monthly average cost of the designated insurance plan(s), which is calculated based on the plan(s) and the length of the term.

Premium Premium

This field displays both the total premium for the term, as well as the number of months of the term.
