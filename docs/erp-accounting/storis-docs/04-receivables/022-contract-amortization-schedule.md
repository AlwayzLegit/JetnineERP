Access

Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Merge/Refinance Contracts > Actions button > View Contract Amortization Schedule

Installment Receivables > Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts > Review Contract Details > Actions button > View Contract Amortization Schedule

This grid displays a row for each of the monthly payments in the entire contract's term. Each row contains a detailed breakdown of the payment.

Grid InformationGrid Information

Reference - If an installment payment has been charged (moved to Open Item Receivables) to the account during cycling, this column shows the reference number for that installment payment.

Due Date - The payment due date displays for each installment payment in this contract.

Principal - The amount of each installment payment applied to the principal displays in this column.

Interest - The amount of each installment payment applied to the interest amount displays in this column.

Insurance - The amount of each installment payment applied to the insurance amount displays in this column.

Late Fees - The amount of any late fees charged displays in this column.

Adjustment - This column tracks all balance adjustments and credit reversals performed via Manage and Adjust Installment Contracts. It also accumulates automatic adjustments applied via credit memos, exchanges for less, and credit dollar only adjustments.

Applied - The amount (if any) that has previously been applied to the payment displays in this column.

Remaining - This column displays the remaining amount left for a particular installment payment in the term. It is the sum of the principal, interest, insurance, late fees, adjustment and applied amount.
